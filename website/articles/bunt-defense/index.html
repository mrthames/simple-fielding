Bunt Defense for Youth Baseball and Softball: Who Charges, Who Covers

A simple bunt defense for youth baseball and softball teams: who fields the bunt, who covers first base, what changes with a runner on second, and why you take the sure out.

5 min read · September 24, 2026

Bunts cause more chaos in youth games than almost any other play. Three players charge, two of them collide, and nobody is at first base. A simple bunt defense fixes most of it, and it only needs one idea: who charges and who covers.

Who charges

  • First and third basemen charge the lines as soon as the batter squares.
  • The pitcher follows through and comes off the mound toward the plate. They take the middle.
  • The catcher takes anything right in front of the plate, and is the one who calls the play ("One! One!") because they're facing it.

Whoever gets there first and has the best angle takes it. The others get out of the way, and the corner who didn't field it gets back toward their base.

Who covers

  • Second baseman covers 1st. This is the key to the whole thing, because the first baseman is charging.
  • Shortstop covers 2nd, or 3rd if there's a runner on 2nd (see below).
  • Outfielders run in to back up: right fielder behind 1st, left fielder behind 3rd, center fielder behind 2nd.
Watch this play
Bunt down the 3rd-base line. The third baseman fields it, and the second baseman covers 1st for the throw.

Take the sure out

A bunt is usually a sacrifice. The other team is trading an out to move a runner. At the youth level, the best answer is to take the out they're giving you at 1st. Trying for the lead runner at 2nd or 3rd means a hurried throw to a moving target, and the ball in right field turns one base into three.

Watch this play
Bunt back to the pitcher with a runner on 1st. The pitcher fields it and takes the sure out at 1st.

Exception: bases loaded and the catcher fields it right in front of the plate. Step on home for the force, then throw to 1st.

Runner on 2nd

Now the third baseman has a decision: charge the bunt or stay home to take a throw at 3rd. Keep it simple for younger players: the third baseman charges, and the shortstop covers 3rd. The second baseman still covers 1st.

Watch this play
Bunt with a runner on 2nd. The third baseman charges, so the shortstop covers 3rd.

Practice it in five minutes

  1. Put nine kids in their spots and have a coach bunt (or roll) the ball to different places.
  2. Before each one, ask the second baseman: "Where are you going?" (First!)
  3. Rotate positions so every kid learns the second baseman's job. It's the one that gets forgotten.

Quick answers

Who covers first base on a bunt?
The second baseman. The first baseman charges the bunt, so the second baseman runs to the bag to take the throw.
Where should you throw a bunt in youth baseball?
Usually to first base for the sure out. A bunt is often a sacrifice, and trying to get the lead runner leads to more errors at younger ages than it saves.
Who covers third on a bunt with a runner on second?
The shortstop, because the third baseman may have to charge the bunt.
