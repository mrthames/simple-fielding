"Where do I go?" — answered for every player, on every play.

Drag the ball from home plate to wherever it's hit. All nine fielders move to their jobs: who gets the ball, who's the cutoff, who covers the base, who backs it up.

Try it now — it's free

Runs in the browser on an iPad, a phone, or a laptop hooked up to a projector. iOS and Android apps are on the way.

A whiteboard that moves

Every coach has drawn circles and arrows on a whiteboard in the dugout. Simple Fielding is that whiteboard, except the players move and you can change the play in a second.

1

Set the situation

Tap the bases to put runners on. Set the outs, and whether the batter hits righty or lefty.

2

Hit the ball

Pick a grounder, line drive, fly ball, pop-up or bunt. Then drag the ball from home plate to where it's hit.

3

Watch everyone move

All nine fielders run to their jobs, and the throw goes where it should. Tap any player to see their job in plain words.

Built for the dugout and the team meeting

It's designed for an iPad first, and works on any phone. Plug a laptop into a projector and use projector mode to walk the whole team through plays before practice. It fits Little League baseball, 50/70 and fastpitch softball fields.

Free, and staying that way

Simple Fielding is built by a dad with kids in Little League, on his own time. There are no ads, no accounts and no subscriptions, and nothing tracks you. It's from the same maker as Simple Pitch Counter.

If it helps your team, you can buy him a coffee. That covers the hosting and the developer fees, and pays for the hours behind the next improvement.
