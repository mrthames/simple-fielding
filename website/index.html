“Where do I go?”
Answered, on the plays that come up most.

Drag the ball from home plate to wherever it's hit. All nine fielders move to their jobs: who gets the ball, who's the cutoff, who covers the base, who backs it up.

Try it now — it's free

Runs in the browser on an iPad, a phone, or a laptop hooked up to a projector. No download needed. iPhone, iPad and Android apps are coming soon.

A whiteboard that moves

Every coach has drawn circles and arrows on a whiteboard in the dugout. Simple Fielding is that whiteboard, except the players move and you can change the play in a second.

1

Set the situation

Tap the bases to put runners on. Set the outs, the batter (righty, lefty or a softball slapper) and how deep the infield plays.

2

Hit the ball

Pick a grounder, line drive, fly ball, pop-up or bunt. Then drag the ball from home plate to where it's hit.

3

Watch everyone move

All nine fielders run to their jobs, and the throw goes where it should. Tap any player to see their job in plain words, and where they should be looking.

Fielding lessons, by age and position

Separate courses for baseball and softball, from a player's first season (T-ball and 8U) up to 13U and 14U. Pick your position, and each play stops at the moment you'd decide: drag yourself where you'd go. You're graded on heading the right way, then you watch the play.

  • Built from how youth coaches teach it: Little League's curriculum, "ready position", "alligator hands", "Ball, base, backup".
  • Talking on defense and sportsmanship are lessons too: calling the outs, picking up a teammate, respecting the umpire.
  • Reads every question out loud for players who can't read yet.
