Third Base Responsibilities: Where the Third Baseman Goes on Every Play

Where the third baseman goes on every play: fielding hard grounders, charging bunts, covering third, and being the cutoff on throws home from left field. For youth baseball and softball.

Third base is the hot corner: the ball gets there fast, so the third baseman needs quick feet and a strong throw across the diamond.

When the ball is hit elsewhere, the third baseman covers third. On a hit to left with a runner trying to score, they're the cutoff for the throw home.

The third baseman's main jobs

  • Field hard ground balls and slow rollers on the left side. With runners on first and second, stepping on third is often the quickest out.
  • Charge bunts down the third-base line.
  • On a single to left with a runner trying to score, be the cutoff for home: in line between the left fielder and the plate.
  • On a ball hit elsewhere, cover third base.

Quick answers

Who is the cutoff on a throw home from left field?
The third baseman, in a common youth system: they line up between the left fielder and home plate. From center and right, it's usually the first baseman.
Should the third baseman charge a bunt?
Yes, on a bunt down the third-base line. With a runner on second, the third baseman may need to stay near the bag so the runner can't take third; the pitcher fields that bunt instead.
Who covers third base on a steal of third?
The third baseman, with the left fielder backing up behind the bag.

Where teams do it differently (who's the cutoff home, who covers second on a steal), these pages follow a common youth system. Your coach's system wins.
