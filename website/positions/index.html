What every position does

The nine defensive positions in youth baseball and softball, what each one is responsible for, and where each fielder goes on every play in the library.

  • PPitcherAfter the pitch, the pitcher is a fielder: covering first on balls to the right side, covering home on a passed ball, and backing up bases on hits. Here's every play, from youth baseball and softball.
  • CCatcherThe catcher sees the whole field: backing up first on ground balls, calling cutoffs, covering home, and directing bunt plays. Here's every play for youth baseball and softball catchers.
  • 1BFirst basemanWhere the first baseman goes on every play: getting to the bag on ground balls, holding runners, charging bunts, and being the cutoff on throws home from center and right. For youth baseball and softball.
  • 2BSecond basemanWhere the second baseman goes on every play: covering second on double plays and steals, covering first on bunts, and going out as the relay on balls to right. For youth baseball and softball.
  • SSShortstopWhere the shortstop goes on every play: fielding the most ground balls, covering second, going out as the relay on balls to left and center, and leading the infield. For youth baseball and softball.
  • 3BThird basemanWhere the third baseman goes on every play: fielding hard grounders, charging bunts, covering third, and being the cutoff on throws home from left field. For youth baseball and softball.
  • LFLeft fielderWhere the left fielder goes on every play: charging ground balls, hitting the cutoff, and backing up third base and the infield. For youth baseball and softball.
  • CFCenter fielderWhere the center fielder goes on every play: leading the outfield, taking fly balls with priority, backing up second base on steals and throws, and backing up the other outfielders. For youth baseball and softball.
  • RFRight fielderRight field isn't where you hide a player: the right fielder backs up first base on almost every ground ball in the infield. Where the right fielder goes on every play, for youth baseball and softball.
