# Google Play listing (Simple Fielding)

Paste into Play Console (Simple Fielding → Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing). Kept in step with
`store/app-store/listing.md`. Written for coaches and parents: the target audience is 13+ and the listing
mustn't read as aimed at children (Families policy), so never "for kids", and no "every play" claim.

**App name (max 30):** Simple Fielding

**Short description (max 80):**

Show your players where to be on every play: cutoffs, covers and backups.

> "On every play" here means each play you pick. If it reads as a claim to cover all plays, use:
> `Show your players where to be: cutoffs, covers, backups and throws.`

**Full description (max 4,000):**

```
Simple Fielding shows your team where every fielder goes, and why, before the ball is even hit.

Pick a situation, tap where the ball goes, and watch the play unfold on a real field: who fields it, who covers
each base, who cuts off the throw and who backs it up. Runners move too, so your players see why the throw goes
where it goes.

BUILT FOR PRACTICE AND THE DUGOUT
- A library of common plays: singles and doubles with runners on, bunts, steals, first-and-third, rundowns and more
- Build your own: set the runners and outs, then tap anywhere on the field to hit the ball there
- Each player's job in a sentence your players can follow
- Slow motion, replay, and a timeline you can scrub
- Save the plays you teach and share them with your team as a link

WHEN IT GOES WRONG
- Bobbles, balls through the legs and throws that get away, timed on the field, with who ends up safe

SEE IT FROM ANY POSITION
- A 3D view from behind home plate, from overhead, or through the eyes of any fielder
- Vision cones show where each player should be looking

LEARN BY DOING
- Lessons ask your players where to go, then show them

YOUR LEVEL, YOUR FIELD
- Baseball fields from coach-pitch up through 90-foot bases, and fastpitch softball
- Add your roster so players see their own names on the field

ON THE BIG SCREEN
- A projector mode sized for a TV or a team meeting

SIMPLE AND PRIVATE
- No accounts, no ads, no tracking
- Works offline
- Everything you save stays on your device

From the maker of Simple Pitch Counter.
```
